# BorrowHub Code of Conduct

## Our standard

BorrowHub contributors must communicate respectfully and work toward a safe, constructive, and technically honest community.

Expected behavior includes:

* Treating people with respect.
* Giving specific and actionable feedback.
* Assuming good intent while addressing harmful behavior directly.
* Welcoming contributors with different experience levels.
* Acknowledging mistakes and correcting them.
* Discussing technical decisions without attacking individuals.
* Respecting privacy and confidential security reports.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not accepted:

* Harassment, threats, intimidation, or discrimination.
* Personal attacks or insulting language.
* Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
* Publishing private information without permission.
* Deliberately disrupting discussions or project infrastructure.
* Retaliation against someone who reports a concern.
* Knowingly submitting malicious code, credentials, or unsafe instructions.

## Scope

This code applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, community spaces, and public interactions representing BorrowHub.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers.

Do not publish sensitive personal details in a public issue.

Include:

* What happened.
* Where it happened.
* Relevant links or screenshots.
* Any immediate safety concern.
* The outcome you are requesting, when appropriate.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently remove contributors when necessary.

Enforcement decisions should consider severity, repetition, impact, context, and community safety.

Reports must be handled as privately as reasonably possible. Retaliation against reporters is prohibited.
